How the picks were chosen

America's Test Kitchen still rates the All-Clad D3 12" its top stainless skillet. Several 2026 tests pick Lodge's Chef Collection for cast iron. de Buyer and Matfer are the reference French carbon steel pans. Le Creuset and Staub lead enameled Dutch ovens.

Prices

Care in one line each

  • Stainless: preheat, then oil, then food. Bar Keepers Friend for stains.
  • Carbon steel and cast iron: rinse, dry on the burner, thin coat of oil.
  • Enamel: medium heat is plenty. No metal scrapers.
  • Nonstick: low-medium heat, no metal tools, no dishwasher.
